Screens on the waterfront.

LocationWashington, DC
TimeframeSeasonal programming
RoleVideo Engineer · Technical Lead
00

Overview

A changing set of outdoor public programs supported through video setup, playback, routing, display management, and practical troubleshooting in waterfront conditions.

Challenge

Outdoor stages and public screens have to remain readable across distance, daylight, weather, large crowds, and event formats that can shift from film playback to festivals and live sports programming.

Response

I supported marquee waterfront events including DC JazzFest, Summer Movie Nights, the Holiday Boat Parade, and Army–Navy programming, adapting the system to the audience, schedule, and physical site each time.

Outcome

A single public destination could support multiple visual identities and production models while maintaining dependable playback and clear sightlines for large outdoor audiences.
