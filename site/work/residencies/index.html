Overview

Resident VJ work across DC and Baltimore venues — AliceAnna Social Club, GoodyRoom, Reckless Shepherd — building venue-specific looks and live content libraries for changing artists, genres, and nights.

Challenge

A residency is the opposite of a one-off: the same room, week after week, with different artists, genres, and energy. The visual system has to stay fresh without being rebuilt every night.

Response

I build venue-specific looks and content libraries banked by energy and genre, then perform them live — reading the room, supporting the artist, and letting the system absorb each night’s lessons.

Outcome

Rooms with a visual identity that regulars recognize and artists trust — and libraries that make every next night faster than the last.
