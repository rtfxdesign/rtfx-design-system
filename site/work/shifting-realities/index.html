Shifting Realities

Thirteen projectors. Three independent worlds.

FormatThree-zone immersive installation
ScopeTechnical conversion
RoleTechnical Lead · Systems Designer
00

Overview

A projection-heavy immersive installation reorganized into three independent experiential zones while preserving a continuous audience journey through the space.

Challenge

The existing 13-projector environment needed to become three logical zones without losing alignment across architectural surfaces, scenic elements, and interactive moments. Each zone required reliable isolation while sharing the same physical installation.

Response

I configured NVIDIA Mosaic and Pixera Director around the new zone architecture, then led signal routing, media-server organization, calibration, blending, and geometry correction across the installation.

Outcome

The converted system supported distinct visual environments — from vehicle-scale projection to a mirrored portal and interactive lounge — while remaining coherent as one walkthrough experience and practical to operate.
