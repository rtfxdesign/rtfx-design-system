Overview

A multi-surface projection environment spanning a Miami façade, pool deck, and interior atrium — mapped geometry, brand content, and live visual sequencing for an Art Basel week program.

Challenge

Three very different surfaces — an exterior façade, a pool deck, and an interior atrium — had to carry one visual program across an Art Basel week, in outdoor conditions, for an audience that never stands still.

Response

I built the mapped projection environment across all three zones, adapted brand and art content to each surface’s geometry and viewing distance, and sequenced visuals live through the program.

Outcome

A single building read as one coordinated visual instrument — architecture, sponsors, and art program sharing surfaces without fighting for them.
